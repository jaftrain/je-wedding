Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know for celebrating with us in Fort Lauderdale.

The weekend

When and where is the wedding?

We will celebrate on Sunday, February 27, 2028, in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. See the schedule for the full day-of timeline and venue details.

What should I wear?

Cocktail attire is recommended. Choose lightweight fabrics for Florida's warm February days, and bring a light layer for cooler evenings.

Are children invited?

Yes, children are invited! We will also be offering a daycare service during the celebration for families who would like to use it.

Travel & logistics

Which airport should I fly into?

Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL) is the closest option, about 15 minutes from the venue. Miami International Airport (MIA) is a good backup and is about a 45-minute ride away. Visit Travel for booking links and hotel information.

Where should I stay?

We recommend the Fort Lauderdale Marriott Harbor Beach Resort & Spa as a convenient home base for the weekend. You can find booking and directions on the Travel page.

How do I get to the venue?

Uber and Lyft are readily available throughout South Florida, making rideshare an easy option from either airport to the venue.

Yes, plus-ones are welcome. Please notify Jon or Elissa ahead of time so we can plan accordingly.

Our registry details will be shared through our Zola guest page. Your presence is the best gift, and we are grateful for your thoughtfulness.
